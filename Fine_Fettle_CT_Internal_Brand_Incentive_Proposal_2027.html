1. Executive proposal

Launch a 90-day, 10-store pilot starting January 2027. Continue individual budtender recognition through SparkPlug, and introduce a separate storewide cash pool earned only when the store increases internal brand sales share and generates verified incremental gross profit.

Who earns the bonus?

All eligible store employees, including managers, share the earned store pool in proportion to eligible hours worked. Retail/HR determines leave, onboarding, minimum hours and payroll treatment.

What triggers a payout?

A store must meet its individualized monthly brand-share growth target, pass a profit-growth safeguard and meet reporting and compliance checks. A positive sales-share result alone is not sufficient.

How is the pool funded?

Proposed modeling assumption: 25% of incremental, validated contribution profit, with a Finance-approved monthly cap and optional payout tiers. The percentage is adjustable in the live simulator below.

How does SparkPlug fit?

SparkPlug remains the monthly individual leaderboard, funded using the existing 0.5% of the previous month's internal-brand sales. The proposed cash pool rewards storewide outcomes instead.

Launch timing: November and December 2026 are for baseline validation, Finance review, communication and training. North Haven opened October 23; its first complete months should inform the January target. No November or December cash payouts are proposed.

December 2026 — finalize

January–March 2027 — pilot

Run monthly targets and payouts after close and audit; share store dashboards; review results at the end of March before renewal or expansion.

How monthly performance would be evaluated

  1. Calculate each store's net internal cannabis brand sales / total eligible retail sales (share).
  2. Compare share against its approved monthly baseline and individual store growth goal.
  3. Confirm internal brand gross profit and model the profit difference relative to displaced third-party sales.
  4. Fund store team pools from a Finance-approved share of verified positive incremental contribution, subject to caps and payroll burden.
  5. Distribute earned pools according to eligible hours recorded during the incentive month; process through payroll.

6. Guardrails & sign-off

Prevent double counting

Keep SparkPlug's 0.5% individual leaderboard allocation separate. No additional per-SKU cash commissions under this proposal. Budget reports show both programs, even though the team incentive is earned on storewide performance.

Protect the economics

Require positive incremental profit. Reconcile promos and returns, flag stockouts, exclude accessories from BOM calculations, and ensure promotions don't manufacture share gains at negative margin.

Make payouts fair

All approved employees, including managers, participate based on eligible hours. HR specifies eligibility, PTO treatment, transfers, active employment requirements, taxes and payroll handling before launch.

Keep targets credible

Use North Haven's completed November/December months to set its first target. Rebase all stores on the same approved methodology. Review promotions, product launches and seasonal changes.

Open approvals

Finance: which cost and profit measure should fund bonuses?

Current estimates compare own-brand fully loaded BOM to other-brand Dutchie booked cost, which are not identical accounting bases. Finance should determine whether to use retail transfer price, consolidated contribution margin, or another consistent incremental profitability measure, plus any payroll tax or variable retail costs.

Retail leadership: how much payout is meaningful?

At the 25% scenario, nine stores hitting initial targets generate a combined team pool of only ~$1,869, before payroll burden. Review expected employee headcounts and eligible hours by store and consider a more motivating profit-backed tier system before rollout.

HR / Compliance: how should cash bonuses be administered?

Review wage-and-hour treatment, required documentation, payroll processing, applicability to managers, and cannabis-related incentive rules. Track the policy and employee acknowledgments centrally.

Operations: can all stores carry the brands reliably?

Check weekly shelf availability and inventory coverage for Comffy, AURA and SAUS before holding stores to growth targets. Document material stockouts and approved adjustments.
